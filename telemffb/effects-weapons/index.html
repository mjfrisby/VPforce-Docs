    1. Weapons Effects¶

    Vibration effects for weapons employment in the combat simulators, in the order they appear under the Weapons section of the Settings tab. The badge lines and sub-setting tables are generated from the application’s settings catalog, so they always match the app.

    IL-2

    IL-2 weapons effects are configured through the IL2 Shake Master group instead — including a dynamic gunfire mode that derives the effect character from shell mass and velocity.

    1.1 Countermeasures¶

    DCS BMS · All devices

    A thump for each chaff or flare release.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Countermeasure Release — Peak intensity for countermeasure release effect

    1.2 Gunfire¶

    DCS BMS · All devices

    Vibration while guns are firing.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Gun Vibration — Peak intenstiy for gunfire effect

    1.3 Weapons Release¶

    DCS BMS · All devices

    A jolt as ordnance comes off the rails or racks.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Weapon Release — Peak intensity for weapons release effect

    1.4 Weapon Effects Direction¶

    DCS BMS · All devices

    Sets the direction of the applied force for the weapons effects — a fixed angle (0–359°), or −1 for a random direction on each event.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Weapon Effects Direction — Direction of applied force for weapons effects Rrange 0-359 or set to -1 for random
