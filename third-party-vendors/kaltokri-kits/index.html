    3. Kaltokri DIY FFB Conversion Kits¶

    Kaltokri offers DIY conversion kits that add VPforce FFB capability to joystick bases and rudder pedal models. All kits feature solder-free assembly using pre-crimped wiring and require VPforce motor electronics.

    Complete Pricing Information

    For detailed pricing breakdown including motor kits, VAT, and shipping costs, see General FAQ - Kit Pricing.

    3.1 Available Kits¶

    3.1.1 Complete FFB Joystick Base¶

    3.1.1.1 RhinoJoystick - DIY FFB Joystick Kit¶

    • Kit Price: 349€ (motor kit sold separately)
    • Features: Dual-axis FFB, pre-assembled gimbal, solder-free assembly, grip compatibility (Virpil, Thrustmaster, WinWing, VKB)
    • Requirements: VPforce 57BLF03 x2 motor kit, joystick grip, PSU, USB cable
    • Optional: Belt covers (+30€), Aluminum grip mount upgrade (+65€)
    • Availability: Generally in stock - ships in 2-3 working days after payment

    3.2 General Information¶

    3.2.1 Kit Characteristics¶

    All kaltokri DIY kits share these features:

    • Solder-free assembly: Pre-crimped wiring eliminates soldering requirement
    • PETG 3D-printed components: Rigid mechanical parts with necessary hardware included
    • VPforce electronics: Uses official VPforce motor kits and USB boards
    • Evening assembly time: Most kits can be assembled in one evening with basic tools
    • Community designed: Based on open-source designs from VPforce Discord community members

    3.2.2 What’s Included in All Kits¶

    • PETG-printed mechanical components
    • All necessary screws, nuts, washers, pulleys, bearings, and belts
    • Pre-crimped power wiring
    • Assembly hardware specific to the conversion

    3.2.3 What’s NOT Included¶

    • Original pedals or joystick base
    • VPforce motor kit (order separately from VPforce)
    • Power supply unit (PSU)
    • USB cables
    • Tools (hex keys, screwdrivers, drills if required)

    3.2.4 Ordering Information¶

    • Availability: Generally in stock - ships in 2-3 working days after payment
    • Contact: See General FAQ or contact kaltokri via VPforce Discord

    3.3 General Information¶

    For common questions applicable to all kits, see the General FAQ:

    • Power supply requirements and recommendations
    • Shipping costs and delivery timeline
    • Payment options (bank transfer, PayPal)
    • Assembly complexity and required tools
    • Warranty and return policies
    • Contact information and ordering process

    3.4 VPforce Motor Kits¶

    All kaltokri conversions require VPforce motor kits ordered separately:

    3.4.1 Available Motor Kits¶

    57BLF03 Single Motor + USB: 179€ + VAT

    • Used in: RhinoMFG, RhinoACE (x2), RhinoOrion (x2), RhinoR1 (x2)
    • Lower torque, suitable for lighter mechanical loads

    86BLF04 Single Motor + USB: 249€ + VAT

    • Used in: RhinoTPR Inside, RhinoTPR Outside, RhinoMFG86
    • Higher torque, suitable for heavier mechanical loads

    57BLF03 Dual Motor + USB: 299€ + VAT

    • Complete dual-axis kit for joystick conversions

    Order: VPforce Controls Website → “Order FFB Kits” section

    Motor Kit Compatibility

    Each kit is designed for a specific motor model. Do not substitute motor types - mechanical mounts and force characteristics are optimized per kit design.
