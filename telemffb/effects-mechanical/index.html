    1. Mechanical & Airframe Effects¶

    Effects driven by the mechanical state of the aircraft (engine vibration, moving surfaces and doors, damage, hydraulics) in the order they appear under the Mechanical\Airframe section of the Settings tab. The badge lines and sub-setting tables are generated from the application’s settings catalog, so they always match the app.

    1.1 Controls Lock¶

    MSFS · All devices

    For aircraft with a control lock (gust lock): while the configured sim variable reports the lock engaged, TelemFFB holds the controls firmly in place.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Controls Lock Simvar — Sim Variable for control locks
    Invert Controls Lock Simvar — Enable if variable ON means Control Lock is OFF

    1.2 Heli Engine/Rotor Rumble¶

    DCS BMS MSFS XP · All devices

    Helicopter engine and rotor vibration, driven by rotor RPM and blade count.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Heli Engine/Rotor Rumble — peak rumble intensity for helicopter engine/rotor effect

    1.3 Afterburner Rumble¶

    DCS BMS MSFS XP · All devices

    Rumble while the afterburner is lit, scaled by afterburner stage where the telemetry provides it.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Afterburner Rumble — Max intensity of afterburner rumble effect

    1.4 Engine Rumble - Shake Telemetry (IL-2)¶

    IL2 · All devices

    IL-2 exports its physics engine’s own computed engine-shake amplitude and frequency. These effects (one for props, one for jets) render that telemetry directly: startup lope, roughness, and damage arrive in the shake automatically. They are an alternative to the RPM-based rumble effects below: enable one or the other, not both.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Shake Boost — Factor applied to “Engine Shake Amplitude” from game telemetry
    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Shake Boost — Factor applied to “Engine Shake Amplitude” from game telemetry

    1.5 Propeller Rumble¶

    DCS IL2 BMS MSFS XP · All devices

    RPM-driven piston-engine and propeller vibration. The two RPM/intensity pairs work together: at the Low RPM value the effect plays at Low Intensity, ramping toward High Intensity at the High RPM value. These are not floor values: below Low RPM (engine start, shutdown) the intensity keeps increasing above Low Intensity.

    Tip

    High-frequency vibration feels stronger than low-frequency vibration at equal intensity, so the High RPM intensity should be set lower than the Low RPM intensity.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Engine Rumble Low RPM — low RPM threshold for low rpm intensity setting
    Engine Rumble Low Intensity — peak intenstiy of engine rumble at low RPM threshold
    Engine Rumble High RPM — high RPM threshold for high rpm intensity setting
    Engine Rumble High Intensity — peak intensity of engine rumble at high RPM threshold

    1.6 Jet Engine Rumble¶

    DCS IL2 BMS MSFS XP · All devices

    Turbine rumble scaled by engine power, at a configurable base frequency.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Jet Engine Rumble — Intensity setting for the jet engine rumble effect
    Jet Engine Rumble Freq — Frequency in Hz of the engine rumble effect

    1.7 Canopy Motion¶

    DCS XP · All devices

    Vibration while the canopy is opening or closing.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Canopy Motion — Peak vibration intensity when canopy is moving

    1.8 Damage Effect¶

    DCS IL2 BMS · All devices

    A short bump in a random direction, at randomized intensity, each time the aircraft takes damage; some hits land harder than others by design.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Damage Effect — Peak intensity of damage effect shake

    1.9 Flaps Motion¶

    DCS IL2 BMS MSFS XP · All devices

    Vibration while the flaps are in motion.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Flaps Motion — Peak vibration intensity when flaps are moving

    1.10 Fuel Boom/Door Motion¶

    DCS · Joystick

    Vibration while the refueling boom receptacle or door is deploying or retracting.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Fuel Boom/Door Motion — Effect while fuel boom/door is deploying/retracting

    1.11 Gear Buffet¶

    DCS BMS MSFS XP · All devices

    Aerodynamic buffet from extended landing gear, growing with airspeed between the low- and high-intensity speeds.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Gear Buffet — Peak intensity for gear drag buffeting effect
    Buffeting Frequency — Frequency of buffeting effect (Hz)
    Min Intensity Speed DCS, BMS, MSFS Minimum intensity (low) speed for gear buffeting effect
    Max Intensity Speed DCS, BMS, MSFS Maximum intensity (high) speed for gear buffeting effect

    1.12 Gear Motion¶

    DCS IL2 BMS MSFS XP · All devices

    Vibration while the gear is in transit, with clunks at the ends of travel.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Gear Motion — Peak vibration intensity when gear is moving

    1.13 Speedbrake Buffet / Speedbrake Motion¶

    DCS BMS XP · All devices

    Buffet while the speed brake is deployed, and vibration while it is moving.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Speedbrake Buffet — Peak intensity for speed brake buffeting effect
    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Speedbrake Motion — Peak intensity for speed brake motion effect

    1.14 Spoiler Buffet / Spoiler Motion¶

    DCS BMS MSFS XP · All devices

    Buffet while spoilers are deployed, and vibration while they move (motion effect currently F-14 only).

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Spoiler Buffet — peak buffeting intensity when spoilers deployed
    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Spoiler Motion — F14 Only, peak vibration intensity when spoilers are moving

    1.15 Stick Shaker¶

    DCS BMS MSFS · Joystick, Pedals

    A stall-warning stick shaker: the distinct high-frequency square-wave shake of the real device, separate from the aerodynamic AoA/Stall Buffeting. In MSFS it triggers from the sim’s stall warning; DCS/BMS use the configurable AoA threshold.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Stick Shaker Intensity — Enable stick shaker stall warning device
    Shaker AoA Threshold DCS, BMS AoA at which stick shaker activates
    Shaker Freqency (Hz) DCS, BMS Freqency in Hz of the effect

    1.16 Tailhook Motion / Wing Fold Motion¶

    DCS · Joystick, Pedals

    Vibration while the tailhook or wing-fold mechanism is deploying or retracting.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Tailhook Motion — Effect while tailhook is deploying/retracting
    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Wing Fold Motion — Effect while wing-fold is deploying/retracting

    1.17 Low Hydraulic Pressure Effect (Experimental)¶

    DCS BMS MSFS · All devices

    Simulates the heavy, sluggish controls of a failing hydraulic system: as the HydSys telemetry value falls from the configured threshold toward zero, the damper, inertia, and friction forces ramp from their normal values toward the levels configured here.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Hydraulic System Threshold — “Hydraulic System Integrity” (HydSys in monitor) value to consider “low”. Forces will start increasing below this value
    Damper — Damper force when hydraulic system pressure reaches 0
    Inertia — Inertia force when hydraulic system pressure reaches 0
    Friction — Friction force when hydraulic system pressure reaches 0

    Note

    Requires the damper/inertia/friction overrides to be enabled, and headroom left in them; if your base forces already sit at 100%, there is no room to increase them. To set the threshold for a new aircraft, observe the normal HydSys value in the Monitor tab and set the threshold below it.

    Warning

    Increase these forces carefully; too much inertia or friction can cause motor instability and a protective shutdown.

    1.18 Vibration from Telemetry (FlyInside)¶

    MSFS · All devices

    For FlyInside helicopters in MSFS: renders the vibration data exported by the FlyInside flight model directly. See FlyInside Helicopters.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Vibration Intensity — Intensity of Helicopter Vibration (from external flight model) Max intensity controlled by Constant slider in Configurator
    Vibration Expo — Force exponential value. 0 = linear gain, at 0.5, it rises 2x as much in the low end. Negative values decrease force up to 1/3x.
