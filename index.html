    1. Welcome¶

    Welcome to the VPforce documentation site!

    1.1 Available Documentation¶

    1.1.1 Rhino Stick Base Manual¶

    The comprehensive guide to the VPforce Rhino FFB joystick base, covering everything from initial setup to advanced configuration and troubleshooting.

    • Getting Started — unboxing, assembly, software installation, and first-time calibration
    • Using the Rhino — day-to-day operation, grip setup, configuration profiles, and FFB gain tuning
    • Game-Specific FFB Settings — per-simulator FFB tips and recommended settings
    • Troubleshooting — hardware diagnostics, USB issues, power faults, and motor fault codes
    • Game-Specific Troubleshooting — sim-specific issues for DCS, MSFS, IL-2, X-Plane, and Falcon BMS
    • Maintenance — belt tensioning, gimbal servicing, and hardware upkeep
    • Known Issues — documented limitations and workarounds

    1.1.2 TelemFFB¶

    The TelemFFB application bridges your simulator’s telemetry data to the Rhino, enabling dynamic force feedback effects that respond to in-game physics.

    • Installation — downloading, installing, and first-run setup
    • Connecting Your Simulator — enabling and auto-configuring each supported simulator
    • Devices & Instances — using TelemFFB with one or more FFB devices
    • UI Overview — walkthrough of the application interface
    • Aircraft Profiles — per-aircraft FFB tuning and profile management
    • VPforce Configurator Integration — pushing Configurator profiles and gain overrides per aircraft
    • Simulator guides: DCS · MSFS & X-Plane · IL-2

    1.1.3 Community Projects¶

    Community-created FFB projects, modifications, and custom builds.

    Joystick Bases

    • TheAmazinGreat 86 Motor FFB Base — high-torque 86-series motor base
    • Community Rhino Builds — Mabo, Protomaker, and Gadroc community base designs with CAD files and BOMs

    FFB Yokes

    • FFB Yoke by YuchenYan — 3D-printable dual-axis FFB yoke with Honeycomb Alpha adapter support

    FFB Pedal Modifications

    • MFG Crosswind FFB Mod — belt-driven FFB conversion for MFG Crosswind pedals
    • Tiger TPR FFB Mod — no-solder, fully reversible FFB mod for Thrustmaster TPR pedals
    • TPR FFB Pedal Mod by Smitty — cable-driven 86BLF04-based TPR conversion delivering ~20 lbf per foot
    • Virpil ACE Pedal FFB Mod — belt-driven 6:1 ratio FFB conversion for Virpil ACE rudder pedals

    Other

    • FFB Trim Wheel — force feedback trim wheel controllable by hand, sim buttons, or autopilot
    • Force-Enabled Collective — DIY helicopter collective with VPforce FFB for smooth, stiction-free damping feel
    • Tips and Tricks — Planetary Gearbox selection, multi-turn encoder guidance, PSU sharing, and other DIY build tips
    • CAD Files — STEP files for Rhino PCB, motors, and planetary gearboxes
