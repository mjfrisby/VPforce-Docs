    1. Ground Effects¶

    Effects for the aircraft’s interaction with the surface, in the order they appear under the Ground section of the Settings tab. The badge lines and sub-setting tables are generated from the application’s settings catalog, so they always match the app.

    1.1 Runway Rumble¶

    DCS BMS MSFS XP · Joystick, Pedals

    Rolling vibration while taxiing, taking off, and landing; intensity follows the surface and speed.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Runway Rumble — Peak runway rumble intensity

    1.2 Touch-Down Effect¶

    DCS BMS MSFS XP · Joystick

    A single jolt at the moment of touchdown, scaled by how hard you land: the force ramps up to Max Force as the landing g reaches Max G’s. Grease it on and you barely feel it; slam it down and you will.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Touch-Down Max Force — Max force that will be used to generate effect
    Touch-Down Max G’s — G loading where max force will be reached

    1.3 Nosewheel Shimmy¶

    MSFS XP · Pedals

    Pedal vibration simulating nosewheel shimmy under braking: it triggers only above a minimum speed and beyond a minimum brake application.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Nosewheel Shimmy — Max intensity of shimmy effect
    Nosewheel Shimmy min brakes — Minimum brake percent required to trigger shimmy effect
    Nosewheel Shimmy min speed — Speed threshold below which the shimmy effect will not be triggered regardless of brakes

    1.4 Steering Friction (Experimental)¶

    MSFS XP · Pedals

    Ground steering feel for the pedals: friction that is heavy at taxi speed and decays as the aircraft accelerates and the nosewheel unloads, plus a steering-gear centering spring.

    Note

    Requires the Friction Override to be enabled; this effect scales the Configurator friction value.

    Sub-setting Sims What it does
    Steering Friction Intensity — Max intensity of steering friction (% of configurator friction value)
    Steering Friction Expo — Controls decay rate of friction with speed. 0 = linear, gradual decay, -1.0 = very quick decay
    Steering Gear Spring — Strength of steering gear spring
