    4.2 Power Supply Requirements¶

    Q: What PSU should I use?
    A: Required specifications: 19V / 10A with 5.5-2.5mm DC connector.

    Recommended Models:

    • Germany: Innergie 180G (Amazon.de link)
    • UK: JHZL Laptop PSU (Amazon.co.uk link)
    • Universal: Meanwell LRS-350-24 PSU (Amazon link)

    Meanwell LRS-350-24 Assembly Required

    Meanwell LRS-350-24 requires custom enclosure and DC cable assembly. Suitable for powering multiple devices (Rhino joystick + FFB pedals). See wiring video and TRC-LRS-350 Enclosure.

    USB Isolators Required for Multi-Device Setups

    When powering multiple FFB devices from a single PSU, USB isolators are essential to prevent ground loop damage. See Powering Multiple FFB Devices from a Single PSU for detailed wiring guidance.

    DC Connector Quality:

    High-quality DC connectors are critical. Low-quality no-name connectors from Amazon may fail under amperage loads. Invest in quality components.

    Recommended Connectors:

    • DC connector cable: Amazon link
    • Fork cable lugs (smallest red): Amazon link

    Share Your PSU Experience

    If you find a reliable PSU that works well, please share your feedback with Kaltokri to help future customers.

    4.3 Shipping and Delivery¶

    Q: What are the shipping costs?
    A: Kits ship via DHL from Germany. Worldwide shipping available.

    • Standard package weight: 5kg
    • Shipping cost calculated by destination country
    • Check DHL pricing calculator for estimates
    • Contact Kaltokri with your country for exact shipping quote

    Q: How long does it take to assemble and dispatch?
    A: All kits are generally in stock and can be shipped approximately 2-3 working days after payment is received.

    • Dispatch timing may vary based on parts availability
    • Payment order determines dispatch priority
    • Special requests may cause minor delays

    4.4 Payment and Ordering¶

    Q: How can I pay?
    A: Two payment methods available:

    1. Bank transfer (preferred): No additional fees
    2. PayPal: Additional payment fees apply

    Order process:

    1. Contact Kaltokri via email or VPforce Discord
    2. Receive invoice with payment details
    3. Payment order determines dispatch sequence

    International Transfer Fees

    Some banks charge excessive international transfer fees ($50+). Check with your bank first. If fees are prohibitive, contact Kaltokri to arrange alternative payment method.

    4.5 Assembly and Complexity¶

    Q: Is it complicated to assemble?
    A: No. Kits designed for straightforward assembly:

    • Skill level: No soldering required. Anyone with basic mechanical skills can assemble.
    • Time required: One evening (3-4 hours typical)
    • Pre-assembly: Most complex components (Rhino Joystick kit) pre-assembled by vendor

    Required Tools:

    • Crimping pliers and wire end ferrules for VPforce motor kit (example tool)
    • Slotted screwdrivers
    • Metric allen key set
    • Small TORX driver (for mainboard)
    • Basic tools (knife, etc.)

    Assembly Assistance

    If you encounter problems during assembly, contact Kaltokri via email or VPforce Discord for assistance. Parts fit tightly but should not require excessive force.

    4.6 Warranty and Returns¶

    Q: Do I have warranty or right of return?
    A: No. These are DIY kits with the following characteristics:

    Nature of Product:

    • DIY kits with procured materials and custom 3D-printed parts
    • 3D printing done by Winger or Kaltokri in PETG (durable material)
    • 3D-printed parts cannot match injection-molded plastic quality
    • Parts assembled and printed to order

    No Warranty, No Returns

    Warranty, return, and exchange are excluded due to custom assembly nature. Vendor provides support for questions and problems that arise.

    Quality References:

    Customer feedback available on:

    • DCS World Forum
    • IL-2 Sturmovik Forum
    • VPforce Discord (additional)
